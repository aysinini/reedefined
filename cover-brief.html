01 — The Theme Issue No. 2 · June 2026
Drift.

The feeling of a month that moved without you noticing. The particular quality of June — long evenings, slow mornings, the sense of being between things. We want an illustration that captures something in motion, something arriving or departing, something that feels like the precise texture of this time of year.

04 — References & Feeling What we have in mind
The feeling
"Like a memory of a summer you haven't had yet."
Something slightly out of reach. Wistful without being sad.
Publications we admire
"Kinfolk, The New Yorker, Monocle, Apartamento."
Not derivative — but the quality of attention those covers pay.
What we don't want
"Photorealism, stock illustration, anything that could be AI."
We check sketches before finals. If there's no process, there's no submission.
Subject matter
"A figure, a room, a city street — something that suggests a life being lived."
Not abstract. We want the reader to feel they're looking into someone's world.
Scale
"Think full-bleed. This should own the space it's in."
The illustration is the cover — not decoration on top of something else.
The one rule
"Make something you would want to read."
That's the only brief that really matters.
