Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: May 2026

Reedefined is committed to protecting your privacy. We never sell your data, never post on your behalf, and you can disconnect any platform or delete your account at any time.

What we collect

When you create a Reedefined account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your taste profile and magazine preferences
  • Content pulled from platforms you explicitly connect (Instagram, TikTok, Spotify, etc.)
  • Usage data such as pages visited and features used

How we use your data

We use your information to:

  • Build and personalise your monthly magazine
  • Power the AI Creative Director to curate your content
  • Send you notifications when your issue is ready
  • Improve Reedefined's features and experience

Platform connections

When you connect a third-party platform (such as Instagram, TikTok, or Spotify), Reedefined accesses only the data you explicitly authorise. We read your content to build your magazine — we never post, like, follow, or take any action on your behalf. You can disconnect any platform at any time from your Settings page.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We share data only with:

  • Our infrastructure providers (Supabase for database, Netlify/Vercel for hosting, Anthropic for AI)
  • Analytics tools to understand how people use Reedefined
  • Law enforcement when legally required

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete all your personal data within 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at hello@reedefined.app to exercise any of these rights.

Cookies

We use essential cookies only — to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@reedefined.app

Data
Deletion.

Last updated: May 2026

You have the right to have your data permanently deleted. We make this simple and honour all requests within 30 days.

How to delete your data

You can request deletion of your Reedefined data in two ways:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • By email: Send a request to hello@reedefined.app

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile, name, and email address
  • All your magazine issues and drafts
  • Your taste profile and preferences
  • All platform connection tokens
  • Your subscriber list and analytics data

What we retain

We may retain anonymised, aggregated data (such as total platform usage statistics) that cannot be linked back to you. We also retain records required by law for up to 7 years.

Platform disconnection

To disconnect a specific platform (Instagram, TikTok, Spotify etc.) without deleting your account, go to Settings → Connections and click Disconnect next to any platform. This immediately revokes our access to that platform's data.

Timeline

All deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently deleted.

Contact

To submit a deletion request, email hello@reedefined.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and we'll process it within 30 days.
