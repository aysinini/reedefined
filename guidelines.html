How to contribute

Reedefined is written entirely by its readers. Anyone can become a contributor, there's no application, no pitch process. You choose your topic, you write, you publish.

Getting started

  1. Sign up (or upgrade your existing account) as a Contributor.
  2. Open the Contributor Portal and start a new column.
  3. Write your title, tagline, and body text, save as a draft any time.
  4. Choose a category so readers can find your writing.
  5. Submit. Your column appears in the Newsstand as soon as it's published.

Guidelines

  • Write about something you genuinely care about, culture, taste, and everything in between.
  • Be honest. Personal opinion is welcome; plagiarism and fabricated claims are not.
  • No hate speech, harassment, or content that could put someone at risk.
  • Photos and links you include should be your own or properly credited.
  • Keep it readable, a strong column is usually a few paragraphs, not a wall of text.
