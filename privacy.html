Reedefined never sells your data, never posts on your behalf, and you can disconnect any platform or delete your account at any time.

01

What we collect

When you create a Reedefined account, we collect the following:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your taste profile and magazine preferences
  • Content pulled from platforms you explicitly connect (TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, Letterboxd, etc.)
  • Usage data such as pages visited and features used
02

How we use your data

We use your information solely to provide and improve the Reedefined service:

  • To build and personalise your monthly magazine issue
  • To power the AI Creative Director that curates your content
  • To send you notifications when your issue is ready
  • To improve Reedefined's features and overall experience
03

Platform connections

When you connect a third-party platform such as TikTok, Instagram, or Spotify, Reedefined accesses only the data you explicitly authorise during the OAuth flow. We read your content to build your magazine — we never post, like, follow, comment, or take any action on your behalf on any connected platform.

You can disconnect any platform at any time from the Connections page in your portal. Disconnecting immediately revokes our access to that platform's data.

04

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We share data only with:

  • Our infrastructure providers (Supabase for database, Netlify for hosting, Anthropic for AI features)
  • Analytics tools used to understand how people use Reedefined — aggregated and anonymised only
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities when legally required to do so
05

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete all your personal data within 30 days. You may also request deletion of specific data without deleting your account by contacting us directly.

06

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@reedefined.app. We will respond within 30 days.

07

Cookies

We use essential cookies only — to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that identify you personally.

08

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at hello@reedefined.app. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
